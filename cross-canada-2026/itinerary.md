# Cross-Canada: 21 Days · Minimal Prairies · Campgrounds

Nanaimo → Nipigon (Superior) → Nanaimo in **21 days**.

**Saskatchewan & Manitoba:** drive through as fast as practical — **one overnight each way in Brandon, MB**. No overnight in Saskatchewan (crossed in a single long day both directions).

**Sleep:** primary pins are **established campgrounds** (paid). Free / informal backups are listed for each stop.

## Overnight Rules

1. Book or arrive early at **campgrounds** in peak season (Banff / Superior).
2. Free backups are last resort — verify iOverlander the day you arrive.
3. No Ontario highway-rest overnighting.
4. Parks Canada Discovery Pass for Banff / some Ontario Parks.

## Why this prairie strategy

Crossing SK+MB needs ~15 h Medicine Hat↔Kenora. Splitting at **Brandon** keeps each blast under ~10 h and avoids a second prairie night (Regina / Swift Current / Winnipeg / Yorkton / Moose Jaw).

---

## Essentials

1. **BC Ferries:** Reserve outbound and Day 21 return.
2. **Book Banff (Tunnel Mountain)** and popular Ontario Parks early.
3. Prairie blast days: leave at dawn, fuel/food without long stops.
4. Verify campground open dates (shoulder season).
