# Cross-Canada Camper Van: 21 Days Relaxed (Ontario / Superior)

Nanaimo → Lake Superior (Nipigon) → Nanaimo in **exactly 21 days**.

**Pace:** A bit relaxed — most days **~2.5–5 h** driving; one return mountain day ~7 h. Free-first overnights. Banff day-use.

**Nova Scotia?** Not on this clock without turning the trip into a grind (~7 h every day both ways, no short scenic days). This version keeps Ontario’s Superior shore as the eastern high point instead.

---

## Overnight Rules of Thumb

1. Prefer free informal / wild / short-term parking with recent check-ins.
2. Keep a paid backup within 30 minutes.
3. Ontario highway rests are daytime-only — not sleep stops.
4. Banff overnight = paid park camping or sleep in Canmore.
5. Arrive before dark; leave no trace.

## Outbound (Days 1–12)

### Rockies & Prairies

#### Day 1 — Island → Kamloops · ~4.5 h
- Ferry → Horseshoe Bay → Hwy 1 → **Kamloops**
- **Sleep (free):** Exhibition Park / Sahali Mall

#### Day 2 — Kamloops → Golden · ~4.9 h
- **Sleep (free):** Golden free RV parking

#### Day 3 — Banff day · ~2.3 h
- Golden → Lake Louise / Banff → **Canmore**
- **Sleep (free):** Canmore informal · **Backup:** Tunnel Mountain (paid)

#### Day 4 — Canmore → Medicine Hat · ~4.8 h
- Via Calgary
- **Sleep (free):** Quiet pull-off / visitor centre / Co-op

#### Day 5 — Medicine Hat → Swift Current · ~2.6 h
- Short prairie hop (keeps MH→Regina from becoming a long day)
- **Sleep (free):** Living Sky Casino / Walmart

#### Day 6 — Swift Current → Regina · ~2.7 h
- **Sleep (free):** Cabela’s Regina

#### Day 7 — Regina → Brandon · ~4.5 h
- **Sleep (free):** Dinsdale Park / streetside

#### Day 8 — Brandon → Winnipeg · ~2.7 h
- **Sleep (free):** Cabela’s / Walmart

#### Day 9 — Winnipeg → Kenora · ~3.1 h
- **Sleep (free):** Kenora Walmart / Co-op

#### Day 10 — Kenora → Ignace · ~3.6 h
- Required Shield split (Kenora→Thunder Bay is ~7 h)
- **Sleep (free):** Tolérance Hill / White Otter Lookout

#### Day 11 — Ignace → Thunder Bay · ~3.6 h
- Waterfront stroll if energy remains
- **Sleep (free):** Mission Island boat launch / Walmart

#### Day 12 — Thunder Bay → Nipigon (turnaround) · ~1.9 h
- Superior highlight day — lookouts, short hikes, early stop
- **Sleep (free):** Nipigon River Bridge / tourist-info area
- **Why here:** Farthest relaxed Ontario turnaround that still leaves nine easy-ish days home

| **15** | Kenora → Winnipeg | ~2.9 | Cabela’s / Walmart |
| **16** | Winnipeg → Brandon | ~2.7 | Dinsdale / streetside |
| **17** | Brandon → Regina | ~4.5 | Cabela’s |

## Why not Nova Scotia on 21 relaxed days?

Amherst RT is ~136 hours of pure driving. Even at a steady **7 h/day** you need ~20 full drive days with almost no Banff-style short days. That fights “a bit relaxed.”  

| Turnaround | Fits 21 relaxed days? |
| --- | --- |
| Nipigon / Thunder Bay (this plan) | Yes |
| Sault Ste. Marie | Possible if several days climb to ~7 h |
| Québec / Nova Scotia | Needs grind pace or more than 21 days |
