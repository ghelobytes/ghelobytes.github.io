# Cross-Canada Camper Van: Nova Scotia Round Trip

Nanaimo → Amherst, NS (via Fundy) → Nanaimo.

**Outbound: ≤5 hours driving per day.**  
**Return: 5 driving days only** (~13–17 h/day). This is a hard grind — two drivers strongly recommended.

---

## Trip Snapshot

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Duration** | **27 days** (22 outbound + 5 return) |
| **Outbound cap** | **≤5 h/day** (OSRM highway estimate) |
| **Return pace** | **5 days total** — ~13–17 h behind the wheel each day |
| **Distance** | ~12,000–12,500 km round trip |
| **Mode** | Camper van — sleep in vehicle |
| **Sleep strategy** | **Free first** (iOverlander informal / wild / truck-stop / lot). Paid only as backup or one Banff “treat.” |
| **Eastern high point** | Fundy day-use + **Amherst, Nova Scotia** overnight |

**Why asymmetric?** Nanaimo → NS is ~80 h of driving. At 5 h/day outbound that is ~22 days. The ~72 h home fit into **5 marathon days** (~14.5 h average). Share the wheel; pre-book the return ferry for the evening of Day 27.

**Apps:** iOverlander (verify overnight legality the day you arrive), GasBuddy, BC Ferries reservations.

---

## Overnight Rules of Thumb

1. Prefer **Informal Campsite / Wild Camping / Short-term Parking** marked free or commonly used overnight.
2. Always have a **paid backup** within 30 minutes.
3. **Ontario rest areas** are generally daytime-only — don’t plan them as sleep stops.
4. Inside **Banff / Fundy**, free overnight is effectively unavailable — sleep outside park boundaries or budget paid camping.
5. Arrive free spots before dark; leave no trace.

## Return (Days 23–27) — five drive days only

These are **marathon days**. Swap drivers, eat in the van, sleep in easy free lots, no sightseeing. OSRM estimates below; real time with fuel/food/border-of-fatigue is longer.

| Day | Drive | ~Hours | Sleep (free idea) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **23** | Amherst → **Montréal** | ~13 h | Verdun / canal lots with recent check-ins |
| **24** | Montréal → **Sault Ste. Marie** | ~14 h | Bellevue Park / Walmart |
| **25** | Sault → **Winnipeg** | ~17 h | Cabela’s / Walmart — longest day |
| **26** | Winnipeg → **Calgary** | ~16 h | Big-box / casino lot (verify) |
| **27** | Calgary → Horseshoe Bay + **ferry to Nanaimo** | ~13 h + ferry | Book an **evening** Horseshoe Bay → Departure Bay sailing |

**Safety:** Two licensed drivers. Do not stack caffeine through a 17 h day alone. If weather or fatigue hits, abort to the next town and accept an extra night — finishing alive beats the calendar.

**Softer bailouts** (each adds a day): overnight Ottawa or Québec on Day 23; Thunder Bay or Kenora on Day 25; Regina or Medicine Hat on Day 26; Kamloops on Day 27 before the ferry.

## Pace Alternatives

1. **9-day return (~7–10.5 h/day)** — same corridor, humane grind; trip ~31 days.
2. **Turn around at Québec City** — shorter outbound; still reaches French Canada.
3. **Keep 5 h/day both ways to NS** — plan on ~6 weeks total.

---

## Essentials

1. **BC Ferries:** Reserve outbound and return sailings (return is **Day 27 evening**).
2. **Parks Canada Discovery Pass:** Banff and/or Fundy day-use.
3. **Water / dump:** iOverlander pins on travel days — top up before each marathon return day.
4. **Ontario overnight:** Not at highway rest areas.
5. **Verify before dark:** Recent iOverlander check-ins beat old descriptions.
6. **Outbound discipline:** Do not “borrow” from tomorrow’s 5 h budget — Ignace, Swift Current, Marathon, and Fredericton exist so you never stack a 5.2–7 h day by accident.
7. **Return reality:** Five days home means ~72 hours of driving. Treat it like a delivery run, not a holiday.
